Description

The General Membership Assembly (GMA) of the Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. – Council of Student Chapters (IIEE-CSC) is a gathering intended to strengthen the connection among the Council, its officers, and accredited student chapters. The GMA is organized to bring together the members and leaders of the Council of Student Chapters to strengthen organizational awareness, recognize the work of the outgoing leadership, present the direction of the incoming Batch 39, and foster unity among student chapters.

Participants

  • IIEE – Council of Student Chapters Elected and Appointed Batch 39 Officers
  • IIEE – Regional Council of Student Chapters Officers and Aspirants
  • IIEE – Council of Student Chapters Affiliated Members

Objectives

  • To provide a comprehensive orientation on the IIEE, the CSC, and its various committees, including their respective roles, responsibilities, functions, and contributions in supporting the development of student leaders and accredited student chapters.
  • To present and recognize the accomplishments and significant contributions of the IIEE-CSC Batch 38 officers, highlighting their programs, activities, initiatives, and milestones throughout their term.
  • To present the plans, programs, projects, and priorities of the IIEE-CSC Batch 39 officers and formally introduce the elected and appointed officers to the IIEE CSC community and accredited student chapters.
  • To strengthen communication, collaboration, camaraderie, and unity among IIEE-CSC officers, Regional Council of Student Chapters officers, and accredited student chapter officers.
  • To inspire student leaders and members to become proactive, responsible, committed, and service-oriented individuals, encouraging them to uphold professionalism, integrity, excellence, teamwork, and dedication.

VENUE: Respective Host Schools of 10 Regions

Description

The Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. (IIEE) is the sole Accredited Professional Organization (APO) for electrical practitioners, recognized by the Professional Regulation Commission (PRC). Founded in 1975 with just 500 members, IIEE has grown to a vibrant community of over 65,487 electrical engineers and master electricians, continuously expanding its reach. Committed to excellence, IIEE strives to deliver high-quality services and resources, fostering professional growth among electrical practitioners and contributing to national development. Its vision is to be the Philippines' most respected technical professional organization.

The Council of Student Chapters (CSC), IIEE's dynamic student arm, serves as a vital link between the national organization and aspiring electrical practitioners. The CSC facilitates the exchange of technical knowledge and industry insights, empowering students in electrical engineering and related fields.

In celebration of the IIEE's 51st Anniversary, the CSC proudly presents the 21st Regional Student Congress, themed "IIEE Brighter 2026: Empowering the Future Illuminating Excellence, Leading with Humility." This event aims to unite students of the Electrical Engineering, Engineering Technology, and Professionals, fostering academic and professional development while advancing the field of electrical engineering and technology across the Philippines.

Participants

  • Electrical Engineering and Technology students from local organizations nationwide
  • Student Delegates and affiliated members from IIEE Council of Student Chapters

Objectives

The objectives of the 21st Regional Student Congress are:

  • To highlight the 25th Regional Math Wizard and the 39th Regional Quiz Show, that aims to uplift the standards of electrical engineering education in the country by promoting creativity and student excellence.
  • To unite students and create a bond with other regions through exchange of thought and knowledge.
  • To share innovative ideas and experiences to fellow Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students across the region.
  • To elect a new set of Regional Council of Student Chapters (RCSC) officers who will provide effective leadership and represent the region in fulfilling the Council's goals and objectives.

Pre - Events

Mega Webinar

This activity enhances the knowledge of the students in the field of Electrical Engineering and Engineering Technology.

EE Tutorial

Now in its second year, this tutorial series continues to serve as a valuable preparation program for participants of the Regional Math Wizard and Regional Quiz Show. Designed exclusively for aspiring quizzers, it provides an opportunity to strengthen their knowledge, sharpen their problem-solving skills, and develop effective competition strategies before taking on one of the flagship events of the RSCon.

National Student Convention

November 24-26, 2026 (SMX Convention Center Manila)

▼

Event Details

Date of Event: November 24-26, 2026

Location: SMX Convention Center Manila

Description

The Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines Inc. (IIEE) is an association of Electrical Practitioners and the Professional Regulatory Commission's (PRC) sole Accredited Professional Organization (APO) of Electrical Practitioners. The group today has 69,000 members, with more joining all the time. The IIEE's mission is to provide high-quality services and products to instill excellence in electrical practitioners and contribute positively to national development. Its goal is to be the best and most respected professional technical organization in the Philippines.

With that, the Council of Student Chapters (CSC) acts as a link to the national organization of Electrical Engineers and Master Electricians, facilitating the exchange of vital information and technical know-how in the field of Electrical Engineering and related disciplines of interest to students. As of now, the CSC consists of 171 student chapters and 14,000 total affiliated members.

The Council of Student Chapters, the student arm of the Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. (IIEE), is organizing the 24th National Student Convention, the most prestigious event of the Council of Student Chapters. The CSC now invites all local organizations of EE and ET students nationwide.

Participants

  • Electrical Engineering (EE) and Electrical Technology (ET) students from local organizations nationwide
  • Student delegates and affiliated members from IIEE Council of Student Chapters
  • IIEE national officers, Electrical Engineers, and Master Electricians
  • Distinguished guests, professional practitioners, and industry leaders

Objectives

The 24th National Student Convention aims to:

  • Bring together Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology student members and non-members of IIEE-CSC from all regions, creating a platform for interaction and exchange of ideas and knowledge.
  • Showcase the winners of the 20th Regional Student Convention quiz competitions, the 38th Regional Quiz Show, and the 24th Regional Math Wizard, who will compete for the 38th National Quiz Show and 24th National Math Wizard. It will also offer the 22nd PEC Quiz Show, the 11th Clash of the Intellects in Engineering Sciences and Allied Subjects, 3rd Electrical Technology Quiz Show, as well as the 1st STEMEfy Quiz Show.
  • Elevate the standards of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology education in the country and ignite enthusiasm among EE students for ESAS and PEC, and among ET students for ETQS by organizing healthy competitions among student members nationwide.
  • Familiarize students with various aspects of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology, including job opportunities, through a series of engaging technical and non-technical webinars featuring topics tailored to the interests of EE and ET students.
  • Provide a platform for Senior High School students to demonstrate their knowledge in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics.
  • Offer students the opportunity to witness the biggest and most prestigious gathering of electrical practitioners and the exhibition of the latest technology at the 50th IIEE Annual National Convention.

Introduction

The Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines Inc. (IIEE) is an association of Electrical Practitioners and the Professional Regulatory Commission's (PRC) sole Accredited Professional Organization (APO) of Electrical Practitioners. The group began with 500 members in 1975 and today has 60,000 members, with more joining all the time. The IIEE's mission is to provide high-quality services and products to instill excellence in electrical practitioners and contribute positively to national development, and its goal is to be the best and most respected professional technical organization in the Philippines. With that, the Council of Student Chapters (CSC) acts as a link to the national organization of Electrical Engineers and Master Electricians, facilitating the exchange of vital information and technical know-how in Electrical Engineering and related disciplines.

The Council of Student Chapters (CSC), the student arm of IIEE, proudly launches the 3rd HolEEdays: Illuminating Communities, Empowering Futures. This year's HolEEdays takes on a meaningful mission: to spark hope and support communities in need through a nationwide fund-raising initiative. With the collective strength of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students, alumni, and regional councils, HolEEdays transforms the spirit of the season into concrete action.

This event highlights the power of unity within the electrical engineering community. Through friendly competitions, collaborative activities, and region-wide engagement, we harness our skills, passion, and camaraderie to generate meaningful impact. Each contribution becomes a spark that lights a path toward brighter, empowered futures for those we aim to serve.

Rationale

The 3rd HolEEdays, organized by the Council of Student Chapters (CSC) of the Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. (IIEE), is a nationwide initiative designed to unite Electrical Engineering (EE) and Electrical Technology (ET) students while promoting academic, professional, and social growth. Anchored on the theme "Illuminating Communities, Empowering Futures", this year's HolEEdays emphasizes service, collaboration, and impact, transforming the year-end celebration into a meaningful movement for communities in need.

The program features four main components:

  1. Volt Vanguard: National E-Sports Cup
    A friendly but competitive gaming event open to students and alumni nationwide. Beyond fostering camaraderie and skills, the tournament serves as the primary channel for fund-raising activity.
  2. HarmonEE: Carols of Hope
    A Christmas carol activity for Regional Council of Student Chapters and School Chapters, fostering camaraderie, holiday cheer, and community while supporting the fundraising and donation drive.
  3. Project Spark: Light the Way Forward
    A component that mobilizes students, alumni, and partners to generate resources for communities affected by natural calamities, particularly in Cebu.
  4. Project HopEE: Rays of Kindness
    An outreach program that translates funds raised into tangible support for affected communities by distributing essential items and assistance to families and communities in need.

Objectives

The 3rd HolEEdays aims to:

  1. To unite EE and ET students, alumni, and chapters nationwide through collaborative and festive activities such as the E-sports tournament and Christmas carol.
  2. To raise funds for outreach initiatives that directly support communities affected by natural calamities, particularly in Cebu.
  3. To provide platforms for skill development, teamwork, and social engagement through competitive and collaborative events, while fostering a sense of community.
  4. To promote a culture of service, generosity, and community impact among students, alumni, and partners, aligning with the theme "Illuminating Communities, Empowering Futures."

Participants

The 3rd HolEEdays is open to a wide range of stakeholders within the Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology community, including:

  1. EE and ET Students from colleges and universities across the Philippines who wish to participate in the E-sports tournament, Christmas carol, fund-raising, and donation drive activities.
  2. Alumni who want to join the tournament, support fundraising efforts, or contribute to outreach initiatives.
  3. Regional and School Chapters (RCSC and SCs) serving as organizers, coordinators, and participants in all activities, including the Christmas caroling event.
  4. Sponsors and Partners supporting the event through sponsorships, donations, or in-kind contributions.

Eligibility

1. Volt Vanguard: National E-Sports Cup

Eligibility Criteria:

  1. Must be a bona fide student in Electrical Engineering (EE) or Electrical Technology (ET), or an alumni of these programs.
  2. Must be currently enrolled (for students) or a graduate (for alumni) of EE or ET program.
  3. Open to both members and non-members of the IIEE-CSC. Non-members are welcome to participate in the tournament and other activities; pricing for non-members may vary.

2. HarmonEE: Carols of Hope

The Christmas Carol segment is open exclusively to:

  1. Regional Council of Student Chapters and School Chapters (RCSC and SCs) of the IIEE-CSC.
  2. Only current student members of each chapter may participate.
  3. Chapters may form teams consisting of 5 to 10 members to showcase their performances; participation also supports fundraising and outreach initiatives.

Event Details

EVENT: 10th Electrical Engineering and Technology Students' Summit (EETSS)

THEME: "Beyond the Spark: Forging a Path to the Infinite Future"

FORMAT: The summit brings together virtual activities and a culminating three-day face-to-face summit.

Description

The Institute of Integrated Electrical Engineers of the Philippines, Inc. (IIEE) is an association of Electrical Practitioners and the Professional Regulation Commission's (PRC) sole Accredited Professional Organization (APO) of Electrical Practitioners. The organization's mission is to provide high-quality services and products to instill excellence in electrical practitioners and contribute positively to national development, with the goal of being the best and most respected professional technical organization in the Philippines.

The Council of Student Chapters (CSC) acts as a link to the national organization of Electrical Engineers and Master Electricians, facilitating the exchange of vital information and technical know-how in the field of Electrical Engineering and related disciplines of interest to students. The CSC consists of 178 student chapters and 15,314 affiliated members, while IIEE has 69,000 members.

The 10th Electrical Engineering and Technology Student Summit is a flagship annual event of the IIEE-CSC, bringing together a dynamic mix of virtual activities and a culminating three-day face-to-face summit. Designed to inspire excellence and unlock the full potential of Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students, the event serves as a platform for learning, innovation, and professional growth—preparing participants to become the future leaders of the electrical engineering industry.

This major nationwide gathering will unite students, professors, industry partners, and practicing electrical professionals from across the Philippines for one grand event where learning, camaraderie, and healthy competition converge. Through engaging programs, collaborative experiences, and industry-driven activities, the summit aims to strengthen connections, spark innovation, and celebrate the shared pursuit of excellence in the electrical engineering community.

Participants

  • Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology students.
  • Professors and educators.
  • Industry partners and practicing electrical professionals.
  • Student teams participating in the summit's competitions and activities.

Major Events

  • 16th Skills Olympics – cultivates technical excellence, innovation, collaboration, healthy competition, continuous learning, and leadership.
  • 1st Basic Occupational Safety and Electrical Safety Quiz Show (BOSHESQS) – tests students' proficiency and awareness in Basic Occupational Safety and Health standards.
  • 1st Research Congress – provides a platform for original research, technical innovations, and developments in Electrical Engineering and Technology.
  • 1st EElimination Design Contest – a technical design competition where students demonstrate proficiency in AutoCAD in developing electrical plans and layouts in accordance with industry standards.
  • 3rd DAGITAB Awards – recognizes outstanding Electrical Engineering and Electrical Technology student chapters nationwide.
  • Golden Night – a formal evening event dedicated to fostering camaraderie and celebrating milestones achieved by the electrical engineering student community.
